About

WHO

I'm a product design leader with a little over a decade behind me, working mostly where B2C habits and B2B constraints collide. AI-powered products, SaaS platforms, enterprise tools, e-commerce.

The industries have been all over the place — EdTech, energy, media, healthcare, IoT, blockchain, agritech. What stays constant is the shape of the job: a business problem someone is unhappy about, looked at through whoever has to live with it.

The part I care about most now is the team. Design thinking workshops, critique sessions, user interview watch parties, and enough transparency that junior designers can see how decisions actually get made.
